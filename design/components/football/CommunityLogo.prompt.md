A community's crest — initials in a rounded square.

```jsx
<CommunityLogo name="Al Shamal" size={64} onHero />
<CommunityLogo name="Al Bahar" size={38} />
<CommunityLogo name="Al Shamal" size={18} />   {/* inline, before the name */}
```

**Square crest = a community. Circle = a person.** Never swap them. Wherever a match names its community, put the small crest before the name — that single 18px mark is most of what makes a screen feel owned.
