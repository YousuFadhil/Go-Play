A participant on a match roster.

```jsx
<ParticipantRow name="Talib Abu Fahd" position="Defender" />
<ParticipantRow name="Faisal" guest />
<ParticipantRow name="Nasser Al Kaabi" position="Forward" reserve index={1} />
```

Never word a guest's name yourself — it is always "Professional (name)", the same on every screen that shows a roster.
