The 54px date block every match card leads with.

```jsx
<DateTile weekday="Thu" day={13} month="Aug" />
<DateTile completed weekday="Fri" day={7} month="Aug" />
```

A match is a moment and a community is a place: lead a match with this, and a community with its `CommunityLogo`. That is what keeps the two card types distinct without tinting either.
